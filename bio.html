Very often, the topic of accessibility in college courses focuses on the instructor's responsibility to create accessible content. But what about student-authored content?

Your assignment this week is to create a quick bio of yourself with the following features enabled in your post:

  1. A picture of yourself, with alternative text applied to your picture.
  2. A heading with your name and the word bio, like: "Jonny Appleseed's Bio."
  3. A short description of yourself with your major.
  4. A list of hobbies (either bulleted or numbered).
  5. A link to something you find interesting, a webpage you built, or a LinkedIn profile.

Here is my example:

Damian Sian's Bio

Hi, I'm Damian! I'm a student pursuing a degree in Information Technology. I'm passionate about web development, accessibility, and creating technology that works for everyone.

Hobbies

  • Playing guitar
  • Attending concerts
  • Gaming
  • Vibe coding
